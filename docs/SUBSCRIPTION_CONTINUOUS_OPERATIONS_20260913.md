## Implemented runtime

- `continuous.py`: transactional hash-chained model-paper journal, explicit parent-head compare-and-swap, atomic event batches, idempotent event/fill IDs, chronological input validation and closed-copy backup. An invalid batch rolls back entirely.
- Decisions retain original information availability, snapshot/config/review hashes, thesis/model version, reason codes, original forecasts and amount bounds. BUY/SELL is not generated by this library. HOLD/WATCH/REJECT decisions are also retained to reduce selected-trade-only evaluation.
- Delivery records bind the exact decision hash. Model fills require a delivery record, a post-delivery calendar-adapter execution window, matching raw-price observation time and calendar receipt. No historical prior-close fill after later publication. Partial fills, funds, oversells, fees and average cost are reconciled. A short-RS-only SELL reason is rejected; general sell authority still belongs to reviewed thesis/risk rules.
- Splits preserve basis and invalidate old quantity instructions. Dividend receivables are fixed at an explicitly ordered ex-date event and paid later; sale after ex does not remove that receivable. Marks require complete held-security coverage, contemporaneous raw prices and no missing-price-as-zero. No automatic total-return/MDD or subscriber return claim.
- Fixed 21/63/126/252-session forecast outcomes are different from actual exit/trade-P&L outcomes. Each binds the original decision and a total-return-index source/calendar receipt. Future/unmatured observations are rejected; earlier-cutoff reports do not use later-recorded outcomes. Outcome overwrites are rejected; corrections require a new versioned replay branch, not edits in place.
- `learning_report` produces horizon/model-separated MAE, forecast bias, Brier score and loss-sign counts. It includes WATCH/REJECT outcomes where present. These are diagnostic sample means, not independent-observation significance claims.
- `calibration_challenger` fits only a shrunk forecast-bias intercept on matured training observations, and compares it on decisions after the training cutoff whose outcomes are mature by evaluation. Split-crossing decisions are purged. A fixed shrinkage penalty and >=30 train / >=10 holdout floors prevent one-trade fitting. Returns are floored at -100%. No new selector, probability-calibration model, trading return, or automatic promotion. Repeated experimentation needs an external preregistered ledger and untouched OOS.
- `run_model_cycle.py` accepts hash-pinned event envelopes and optional hash-pinned closed parent DB, writes a NEW run, replays an atomic batch, snapshots the model journal, and produces learning/challenger reports. It never overwrites the parent snapshot or advances a Drive accepted pointer. A failed run may leave a working directory for diagnosis but has no success manifest and must not be published.
- `operations.py`: persistent issuer/security update tasks, lease recovery/fencing, exponential retry, whitelisted error codes, event-version deduplication and completion ONLY after matching produced/restored bytes. No actual data downloads or scheduler activation are performed here.

## Trust and accounting boundaries

A hash-shaped source/review/delivery/calendar receipt is caller-provided provenance, not authentication of a provider or semantic review. The future integration adapter must authenticate these receipts, verify official session adjacency (including holidays/early closes), freshness and corporate actions, and supply the fixed implementation-window policy before real service use. Synthetic fixtures deliberately use illustrative calendars and prices. Their success is not real quote/market admission. All summaries retain orders_allowed=false and commercial_release_allowed=false.

Initial ledger adapter: USD, long-only model paper, fractional units allowed, average cost, no customer deposits/withdrawals or margin. No tax lots, shorting, options, cross-currency settlement, rights issues, spinoffs, mergers, cash-in-lieu or delisting settlement. Unknown event types fail closed. This limitation does not remove the affected companies from research. A new validated corporate-action adapter is required before using those events in a model book.

A MARK valuation includes cash, market value and accrued dividends; realized P&L separately includes fees and allocated basis. It is not an annualized performance report. Execution fees are explicitly charged; slippage is reflected in the supplied executable price, not blindly charged twice. An external execution model still needs liquidity, spread and impact validation.

SQLite is used on one local/cloud worker. Persist closed snapshots to the approved private Drive archive, then restore and compare bytes. Queue leases fence stale local workers; they are not a cross-repository/global object-store lock. Cross-worker publication still needs a single approved publisher plus conditional pointer update and an authenticated storage receipt.

## Operating cycle after reviewed integration

1. Restore and verify last successful data/model/queue snapshots; detect missing expected sessions before processing new ones.
2. Use existing scheduler/collectors, not a parallel daily service. Prices: expected completed market session. Fundamentals: new accession/amendment only. Estimates: same fiscal-period identity and provider definition. Qualitative work: changed evidence plus all holdings and rotating under-reviewed candidates.
3. Preserve every security and missing-data reason. Expire stale thesis reviews without calling that an economic failure. Do not change historical facts retroactively.
4. Existing reviewed selector/quality/valuation/risk functions produce proposed decisions. Exposure/turnover/cash come from those contracts, not from this lifecycle module.
5. Record review and actual delivery; simulate only after declared eligible execution window. Apply corporate actions and reconcile cash/positions. No broker order path.
6. Store immutable run objects, restore/readback verify, then conditionally advance the single accepted pointer. Failed writes/expired workers do not mark a task complete. Missed jobs must be visible independently of successful run logs.
7. Evaluate forecasts only when their observation horizons mature. Log data errors, selection errors, sizing/timing errors and thesis changes separately from simple losses. Preserve rejected alternatives and opportunity costs.
8. Generate a challenger proposal, require independently reviewed chronological OOS/cost/risk/coverage evidence, then controlled shadow comparison. No automatic champion replacement, no loss-driven instant weight tuning.

Cadence remains daily signals/health, weekly business/learning review, biweekly target review, monthly attribution, quarterly moat/model drift review, and event-driven exceptions. No forced daily trading. Existing scheduler is NOT connected or activated by this PR.

GitHub documents that scheduled events can be delayed/dropped and only run from default-branch workflows: https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/workflows-and-actions/events-that-trigger-workflows . A green/offline PR job is not continuous data freshness or subscriber delivery.

### Reusable lesson

Do not train on generic exit labels, same-date forward-period returns or grade-loss rates as though they were actual fill outcomes. A profitable trade is not automatically a good decision; a loss is not automatically a bad decision. Preserve original inputs, economic intervals, nontrade alternatives, failures and source versions, and compare proposed repairs out of sample before policy changes.
