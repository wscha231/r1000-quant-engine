## TSM — Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing

- Listing verified: NYSE ADS ticker TSM. The issuer's [FAQ](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/faq)
  distinguishes these from Taiwan-listed 2330. Do not buy both lines or treat
  USD trading as removal of Taiwan operating exposure.
- Reported evidence: Q2 2026 revenue rose 36.0% year over year; diluted earnings
  were NT$27.25 per common share and US$4.31 per ADR. Nodes of 7 nm and below
  generated 77% of wafer revenue. These support a current advanced-process demand
  hypothesis, not proof that competition or cyclicality has disappeared.
  [July 16 company release](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/encrypt/files/encrypt_file/reports/2026-07/a80d7933be643644081584087731f73b22ea5a2c/2Q26%20EarningsRelease.pdf).
- Next checks: independent customer qualification and yield evidence, Samsung
  and Intel alternatives, packaging capacity, overseas-fab economics, customer
  concentration and Taiwan disruption scenarios. Monitor realized margin and
  free cash flow after capacity spending, not just process-node announcements.
- Valuation: native TWD financial statements require dated conversion and
  verification of the ADS/common-share basis before comparison with TSM's USD
  quote. Preserve dividend payment dates, withholding and depositary charges.
  No current price target or preferred portfolio weight has been approved.

## ASML — registered NASDAQ ordinary shares

- Instrument correction: the issuer describes registered NASDAQ ordinary
  shares, USD, alongside its Euronext line. An ADS ratio from a different company
  cannot be applied. [Official share structure](https://www.asml.com/en/investors/shares).
- Reported evidence: Q2 sales were EUR9.326 billion, including EUR2.762 billion
  from installed-base services and field options; gross margin was 54.0%.
  Service/upgrade revenue helps test recurring economics. Planned EUV/DUV
  capacity expansion is a forecast, not delivered demand or a guaranteed return.
  [July 15 results](https://www.asml.com/en/news/press-releases/2026/q2-2026-financial-results).
- Next checks: customer utilization and tool acceptance, high-NA productivity
  and cost per wafer, competitive substitutes, supplier concentration, export
  restrictions, order deferrals and cash conversion. Compare technical leadership
  with realized customer economics rather than assigning an automatic moat score.
- Governance: assess the protective preference-share option and the resulting
  voting implications using the issuer's share-structure disclosure above.
  Valuation uses EUR financials and USD trading prices with a dated conversion.
