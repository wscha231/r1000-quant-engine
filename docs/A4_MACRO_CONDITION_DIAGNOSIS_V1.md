# A4 observed macro conditions

This extension explains the original-bound current macro panel. It is research
context and does not replace the incumbent ten-axis risk readiness contract or
the externally reviewed historical relationship release.

`macro_diagnosis.windows` contains SHORT, MEDIUM and LONG descriptive lookbacks:
daily5/21/21 source observations, weekly1/4/13 weeks, monthly1/3/12 months.
Horizon1/5/10 references SHORT,21/63 MEDIUM,126/252 LONG. These references are
presentation context, not estimates of returns over those future horizons.
`horizon_outlook.direction` continues to require the existing reviewed release.

Each family retains its measures, actual basis/unit, observation and availability
dates, original/normalization hashes, missing members and contrary directions.
Families are neither averaged nor counted as independent equity signals.
Missing, stale, future or incomplete-window evidence never becomes neutral or
an older successful observation. Macro readiness is not completion of A4.
H.10 broad-dollar evidence separately retains daily observation and weekly
publication frequencies. Its expected complete business week and next release
deadline are rechecked when explaining a summary at a later cutoff. Scheduled
release times are context, not actual publication or availability evidence.

- Funding spread increases describe tightening pressure; shared rates overlap.
- Reserves rising and TGA falling describe easing balance conditions separately.
  No balance-sheet components are summed or compared with a fixed reserve cutoff.
- Deposit, loan and cash-asset changes use EXPANDING/CONTRACTING quantity
  labels, with MIXED for contrary changes. They identify neither funding or
  credit easing, stock/MMF transfers nor loan quality; H.8 scope is unchanged.
- Payroll growth and declining unemployment use STRENGTHENING/WEAKENING. They
  do not establish a favorable policy response or an equity-return effect.
- Nominal/real yield increases describe discount-rate pressure. Dollar changes
  use APPRECIATING/DEPRECIATING without a financing or equity-return effect.
- CPI changes are displayed separately without an invented equity-return sign.
  Consumption gaps and the existing SLOOS status remain explicit.

Native readiness, missing qualified learning/release evidence and their owner
routes remain visible under `completion_blockers`. No accepted model, A3/A5
allocation, orders, automatic promotion, supplier rights or PIT is authorized.

The first direction-research focus is SPY (S&P500 ETF proxy) and QQQ
(Nasdaq100 ETF proxy, not the Nasdaq Composite). `benchmark_outlook` keeps
1/5/10/21/63/126/252 sessions separate. Without the existing independently
reviewed fixed release, every target/horizon remains INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.
A qualified SPY/QQQ subset may use the existing research fit while other
targets remain insufficient in the unchanged fourteen-target testing family;
the full native-risk/whole-system readiness contract is separate.

On a reviewed release, UP/DOWN/FLAT/MIXED describes agreement of the actual
return-estimate signs. TAILWIND/HEADWIND separately describes contributions
relative to the training mean. A positive expected return can therefore have
a macro headwind. Opposing feature estimates remain MIXED. No magnitude is
averaged, no correlated features become independent votes, and an OOS hit rate
is never presented as the current probability of an increase. Each released
return estimate retains its feature, trial, OOS hit rate and baseline, their
difference, MSE improvement, BY q and independent-window count. The incumbent
purged expanding fit, all negative/insufficient tests, maturity and original
rights/PIT/release gates are unchanged. Raw index versus total-return/proxy
price bases still require their original input evidence.

`qe_assessment` exposes observed balance, administered-rate, employment and
inflation changes with original provenance, alongside explicit missing dated
FOMC/implementation and policy-episode evidence. These are inputs for future
policy-response research, not a fitted QE-start likelihood. Current drivers
cannot establish an official policy state, a QE probability or equity direction.
The Fed's [2019 implementation statement](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20191011a.htm)
identifies reserve-management purchases as technical measures; its
[March2020 statement](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20200315a.htm)
specifies substantial Treasury/MBS purchases. They are definition references,
not verified training originals, a historical event-label archive or current
policy evidence. Preserve purchase purpose, instrument and net-purchase versus
reinvestment distinctions. TGA redistribution, repos/emergency lending, QT
slowdown/cessation and reserve-management purchases must remain separate from
QE episode labels. Repeated days/weeks within a policy episode are not
independent QE events. QE-start probability stays null until independently
reviewed event history and out-of-sample calibration exist.
